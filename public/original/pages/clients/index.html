But I have a question.

Console
Is this a real skylight or is it a lighting technology?

SUNROOOF is not a traditional skylight. It’s an advanced lighting technology that replicates the look and feel of natural sunlight.

Does SUNROOOF change colour according to the time of day?

Yes, it shifts from warm in the morning to bright midday and warm again in the evening, mimicking natural sunlight.

Where is SUNROOOF manufactured?

Designed in Germany, 80% assembled in India ideal for Indian homes and commercial spaces.

How does SUNROOOF improve my space?

It recreates an open-sky feel in low-light areas like basements or apartments.

Is SUNROOOF similar to a LED light?

SUNROOOF uses advanced LED tech, nanotechnology, and AI to simulate distant natural light.

Does it get dark patches like LED lights?

No, the light feels soft, balanced, and evenly diffused just like natural daylight.

What happens at night with SUNROOOF lighting?

It stays off unless manually controlled for warm, dim night time ambiance.

Does this light provide Vitamin D?

Not yet under development for a future version by 2026.

Does SUNROOOF offer any health benefits?

Yes, it enhances mood, focus, and sleep like natural sunlight.

Do you sell only the light console?

No, SUNROOOF is a complete system including light, woodwork, and automation.

Can it be integrated with my existing automation system?

Yes, it works with systems like Casambi.

What is the power consumption?

Each console uses 20W to 50W.

What type of wood is used?

Termite-resistant treated pine wood.

What type of glass is used?

Lacquered Toughened Glass for strength and safety.

Is SUNROOOF the same as Stretch Ceilings?

No. Unlike stretch ceilings, SUNROOOF simulates real natural sunlight.

Installation
How long does the installation take?

2 months manufacturing + 2–3 weeks installation.

How can it be cleaned?

Like ceiling lights or fans. For issues, our team will visit.

What is required from the customer to install the product?

An RCC ceiling without false ceiling. You handle false ceiling post-installation.

How long does it take for your team to visit in case of failure?

Within 4–5 working days.

What is the minimum height required to install SUNROOOF?

8 feet. If your ceiling is lower, we recommend our window-style options.
